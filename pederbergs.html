Our Story

Your Hometown Bar

Pederberg's Bar & Grill sits in a historic building right on 3rd Avenue in downtown Clear Lake. Owned and operated by Tommy and Lisa Lundberg, it's the kind of place where everyone knows your name and nobody's in a hurry to leave.

Whether you're stopping in for a cold one after work, grabbing the famous Biker Burger for lunch, or settling in for the evening with friends, Pederberg's is where Clear Lake comes together. Good drinks, good food, good people.
