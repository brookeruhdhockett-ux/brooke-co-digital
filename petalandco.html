Our Story

✽

A flower truck, a dream, and a whole lot of peonies

🚐🌸

Petal & Co.

Blooms on the Go

Petal & Co. started the way most good things do: with a big idea, a little courage, and a vintage truck that needed a whole lot of love. We are a mobile flower shop rooted in Magnolia Springs, Tennessee, bringing farm-fresh stems, dried arrangements, and custom florals to our community one stop at a time.

Whether we are parked at the Saturday market, pulling up to a backyard wedding, or popping up at your favorite coffee shop, our mission is the same: to make beautiful flowers feel easy and accessible. No fuss, no pretension, just gorgeous blooms wrapped with care and handed over with a smile.

Every bouquet is designed by hand using seasonal, locally sourced flowers whenever possible. We believe in slow florals, supporting local growers, and making someone's Tuesday a little brighter.
