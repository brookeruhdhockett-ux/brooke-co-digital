Our Story

The Cornerstone of Canby

For over thirteen years, Korner Stone Kravings has been the place where Canby starts its day. What began as a dream to bring fresh-baked goodness to a small Minnesota town has grown into a gathering place that feels like home.

Every morning before the sun is up, we're in the kitchen — shaping loaves, rolling pastries, and brewing coffee. It's honest work, and we wouldn't have it any other way. This isn't just a bakery. It's where neighbors catch up, farmers grab their first cup, and families make Saturday morning traditions.
