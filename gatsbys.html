A Hidden Gem in Little Europe

Tucked along Main Street in Hendricks, Minnesota, Gatsby's Speakeasy & Supperclub brings prohibition-era charm to the heart of lake country. Step through the door and leave the ordinary behind.

From hand-cut steaks and fresh seafood to craft cocktails mixed with care, every evening at Gatsby's is an occasion. We're a gathering place for locals and lakeside visitors alike — where the food is honest, the drinks are strong, and the atmosphere takes you back to a time when supper was an event.

Whether you're celebrating a special night or just escaping the everyday, you'll find a seat at our table.
