Soda Corral

Pop-Up Soda Bar, Small Town Heart

Soda Corral is a cowgirl-themed pop-up dirty soda and drink bar based in Clear Lake, South Dakota. We set up behind Florals & Finds and bring the good vibes, one handcrafted cup at a time.

From our signature Cowboy and Cowgirl sodas to lotus energy drinks and build-your-own creations, every drink is made to order with real purees, premium creams, and a whole lot of love.
