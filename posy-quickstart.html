The One Rule

Talk to AI like you're talking to a really smart new employee. Be specific. Give context. Tell it what you want back. The more detail you give, the better the answer.

Try These Today

Start Here

Write a Product Script

Instead of staring at your phone trying to think of what to say, let AI draft it and you make it sound like you.
Copy and paste this:
"I'm a TikTok Shop affiliate. I need a 30-second script for [product name]. My style is casual and relatable, like I'm talking to a friend. Include a hook in the first 2 seconds, one key benefit, and a soft call to action. Don't make it sound salesy."
Try This

Find Your Tax Deductions

Most creators leave thousands of dollars on the table because they don't know what counts as a business expense.
Copy and paste this:
"I'm a content creator and TikTok Shop affiliate working from home. I spend money on [ring lights, products to review, internet, phone, etc.]. What can I write off on my taxes? Give me a simple list with a one-line explanation for each. I'm not an accountant — keep it simple."
Try This

Plan Your Content for the Week

Stop waking up every day wondering what to post. Let AI map out your week in 2 minutes.
Copy and paste this:
"I'm a TikTok creator who posts [how many] times a week. My niche is [your niche]. Give me a 7-day content plan with a specific video idea for each day. Include the hook for each video. Mix trending formats with evergreen content."
Try This

Analyze What's Working

Paste your recent video stats and let AI tell you what the patterns are — instead of guessing.
Copy and paste this:
"Here are my last 10 TikTok videos with their view counts: [paste your data]. Which ones performed best? What do the top performers have in common? What should I do more of and what should I stop doing? Be honest."
Try This

Write a Brand Pitch

Reaching out to brands is awkward. Let AI write the first draft so you just have to tweak it.
Copy and paste this:
"Write a DM I can send to a brand I want to work with. I'm a content creator with [your follower count] followers on TikTok. I want to pitch [brand name] for a paid collaboration. Keep it short, professional, and confident — not desperate. Max 4 sentences."
Try This

Get Unstuck on Anything

This is the one prompt that changes everything. Use it whenever you're stuck, overwhelmed, or don't know where to start.
Copy and paste this:
"I'm a [your role] and I'm stuck on [your problem]. I've tried [what you've tried]. I need a simple, actionable plan to move forward. Give me 3-5 specific steps I can start today. Talk to me like a smart friend who's been through this."
