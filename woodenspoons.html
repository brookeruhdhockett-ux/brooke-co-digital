Our Story

A bakery built on
Main Street heart

In the charming lake town of Hendricks, Minnesota — nestled in the area locals call "Little Europe" — Wooden Spoons Bakery brings fresh-baked goodness to the community. From golden loaves of bread to trays of warm cookies and bars, everything is made from scratch with the kind of care that only a small-town bakery can offer.

Step inside, grab a cup of coffee, and feel right at home. Whether you're picking up rolls for Sunday dinner or treating yourself to a fresh-baked cookie, there's something here for everyone.

"Good bread, good people, good morning."
