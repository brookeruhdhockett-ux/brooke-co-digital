Our Story

More Than Just a Coffee Shop

Nestled on 3rd Avenue in downtown Clear Lake, August 17th is the kind of place where mornings feel right. Jon and Mary built this shop to be exactly what a small town needs — a warm, welcoming spot where neighbors catch up over fresh coffee and a good breakfast sandwich.

We brew Dark Canyon Coffee, make our sandwiches fresh to order, and keep our prices honest. Whether you are grabbing a quick cappuccino on your way to work or settling in with pancakes and a newspaper, there is always a seat and a smile waiting for you.
