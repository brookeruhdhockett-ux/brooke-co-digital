Course Overview

The Posy Academy

A course teaching creators how to use AI to run their business — from basic prompts to fully automated AI employees.

The Concept

"I'm a TikTok Shop affiliate with 8 employees working for me. They never sleep, work all night, and I don't pay them a dime."

Starter teaches you to use ChatGPT and Claude like a power tool. Pro gives you pre-made AI employees that manage your money, email, and Lark — deployed to a cloud server and managed from your phone.
