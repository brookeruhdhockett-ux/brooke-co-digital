Questions?

Frequently asked



Do I need any tech skills?
Not at all. If you can use TikTok and send a text, you can do this. Starter is all about learning to use AI through conversation — no coding, no terminal, nothing technical. Pro uses the Posy Setup Wizard, which walks you through everything step by step.
What's the difference between Starter and Pro?
Starter teaches you to use AI as a tool — better prompts, smarter workflows, more time back. Pro goes further and sets up AI employees that actually run parts of your business for you, 24/7. Think of Starter as learning to fish. Pro is hiring a fleet of fishing boats.
What are AI employees?
AI employees are automated agents that handle real tasks in your business — monitoring your brand inbox, tracking your income and expenses, catching important Lark notifications. They run 24/7 on a cloud server and message you on Telegram when something needs your attention. They're not chatbots. They actually do the work.
How much does it cost to run the AI after the course?
About $15-30 per month total. That covers the cloud server ($6-12/mo), AI usage through the Anthropic API ($5-20/mo), and Telegram is free. Less than a streaming subscription for an employee that never sleeps.
Is this only for TikTok Shop creators?
No. The AI skills and automation systems work for any content creator — Instagram, YouTube, Amazon affiliates, brand ambassadors, anyone making content and money online. The Lark Agent is TikTok-Shop-specific, but everything else applies across platforms.
What if I want to upgrade from Starter to Pro later?
You can upgrade anytime and only pay the difference. Your progress carries over — you just unlock the Pro modules and get access to the setup wizard and pre-made agents.
