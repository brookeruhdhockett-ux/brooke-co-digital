How Setup Works

The Posy Setup Wizard handles everything. No terminal. No coding. About 15 minutes.

1
Create your AI account
Sign up at Anthropic (the company behind Claude). Add billing. Takes 3 minutes.
2
Create your Telegram bots
Message @BotFather on Telegram and follow the guided steps. 60 seconds per bot.
3
Run the Posy Setup Wizard
Go to setup.posyacademy.com. Enter your API key and bot tokens. Pick your agents. Click deploy.
4
Connect your services
Click "Connect Gmail" for your email agent. Click "Connect Lark" for your Lark agent. Standard sign-in flow.
5
Say hi to your new team
Your agents message you on Telegram within minutes. They're live, they're working, and they don't take days off.
