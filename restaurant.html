Our Story

Rooted in the Prairie,
Refined for the Table

The dining room at dusk

Three generations of recipes,
one shared table

Iron and Grain Kitchen began with a simple belief: the best meals come from the land closest to you. We partner with South Dakota farmers and ranchers within a 100-mile radius, bringing their harvests straight to your plate.

Our head chef, Marcus Holm, grew up on a cattle ranch outside of Clark. The recipes he cooks today are the same ones his grandmother perfected in her farmhouse kitchen, reimagined with modern technique and deep respect for the ingredient.

The space itself tells a story too. Reclaimed barn wood, hand-forged iron fixtures, and a grain silo converted into our private dining room. Every detail honors the landscape we call home.

More Than a Meal

Every Friday

Live Music at the Hearth

Local and regional artists perform acoustically in our main dining room every Friday evening from 7 to 10 PM. Expect folk, bluegrass, and Americana — the kind of music that belongs in a room with good food and warm lighting.
