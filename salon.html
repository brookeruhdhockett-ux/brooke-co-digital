Our Story

Rooted in community, crafted with care

Ember & Sage was born from a simple belief: every person deserves a space where they feel seen, valued, and completely at ease. Founded in 2019 by two lifelong friends, our woman-owned salon has become a gathering place for the Brookings community.

We blend modern technique with genuine warmth, creating an experience that goes far beyond the chair. From your first consultation to your final look in the mirror, every detail is intentional.
