Our Story

✦

A little studio with a whole lot of heart

💅✨

est. 2024

Where Self-Care Meets Small Town Charm

Blush & Polish started with a simple idea: every person deserves a space where they feel pampered, seen, and a little bit fancy. Tucked into our cozy downtown studio, we created a place that feels more like visiting a friend than a salon.

Whether you are here for a quick gel refresh before date night, a full set of custom nail art for your bestie's wedding, or just an hour of quiet with a good pedicure, we are here for all of it. Every set is tailored, every detail is intentional, and every visit ends with you feeling like your absolute best.
