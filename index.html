About Prairie Built

Three Generations of Building Right

Prairie Built Construction was founded by Mike and Sarah Larsen right here in Clear Lake. What started as a two-person roofing crew has grown into one of Deuel County's most trusted full-service contractors.

We know what South Dakota weather does to a home. We've seen the hail, the wind, the freeze-thaw cycles that crack foundations and tear off shingles. That's why every project we take on is built to last -- not just built to code.

What We Do

Built to Handle Anything the Prairie Throws at It

From new builds to storm repairs, we bring the same level of craftsmanship and care to every single job.

▲

Roofing

New installations, tear-offs, storm damage repair, and insurance claim assistance. We use materials rated for South Dakota's Class 4 hail zones and 90+ mph wind ratings.

■

Remodeling

Kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and whole-home renovations. We help you modernize your space while keeping it functional for real life with real families.

▬

Concrete

Driveways, patios, garage floors, and foundations. Our concrete work is engineered with proper reinforcement and drainage for South Dakota's brutal freeze-thaw cycles.

▦

Decks & Outdoor

Custom wood and composite decks, pergolas, and outdoor living spaces. We design and build outdoor areas you'll actually use from May through October.
