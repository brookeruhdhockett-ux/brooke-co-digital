Our Story

Something New for Watertown

◆
Interior photo

When Kyle Lalim envisioned The Local, the goal was simple but ambitious: bring something genuinely new to Watertown. Not another chain. Not another sports bar. Something that makes you slow down, sit a little longer, and actually savor the evening.

The Local is a dinner-only restaurant and bar built around the idea that great food and thoughtful cocktails can exist right here in the heart of South Dakota. The menu features dishes and flavors not traditionally available in the area, designed to surprise, delight, and tantalize without pretension.

With a brand new bar, a carefully curated atmosphere, and a kitchen that takes real pride in every plate, The Local is Watertown's invitation to experience something a little different, right in your own backyard.

— Kyle Lalim, Owner

"Designed to surprise, delight, and tantalize — with menu selections not traditionally available in the area."
