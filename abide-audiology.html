questions

frequently asked



How do I know if I need a hearing test?
If you're asking people to repeat themselves, turning the TV up louder, or struggling in noisy environments, it's worth getting checked. We recommend baseline hearing tests for adults over 50 — even if you think your hearing is fine.
Do I need a referral from my doctor?
In most cases, no. You can call us directly and schedule an appointment. Some insurance plans may require a referral — if you're unsure, we'll help you figure it out before your visit.
How long does a hearing evaluation take?
Plan for about 60 minutes for your first visit. We don't rush — we want to do a thorough evaluation and have time to answer all your questions.
What brands of hearing aids do you carry?
We work with all major manufacturers including Phonak, Oticon, ReSound, Starkey, and Widex. We're not locked into any single brand — we recommend what's best for your specific hearing needs and lifestyle.
How much do hearing aids cost?
Hearing aid prices vary widely based on technology level and features. We offer options at every price point and can work with your insurance. We also offer financing to make better hearing accessible.
Do you see children?
Yes. We provide pediatric hearing evaluations in a gentle, kid-friendly environment. Early detection of hearing loss is critical for speech and language development.
