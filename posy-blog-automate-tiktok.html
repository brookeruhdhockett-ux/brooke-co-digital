the 3 things i'd automate first if i was starting my TikTok Shop over

by brooke | the posy academy

if someone took away all my AI tools tomorrow and said "start over," i wouldn't panic.

i'd set up three things. in this order. and i'd be back to running at full speed within a week.

these are the three setups that gave me the most time back, made me the most money, and were the easiest to get running. if you're doing everything manually right now, start here.

1. script + caption assistant

the problem: staring at a blank page before every video, spending 30-45 minutes writing a script that should take 5.

the fix: a permanent AI chat that already knows your niche, your voice, your products, and your style. you tell it what you're filming and it gives you a script that sounds like you — not like a robot, not like a generic influencer, like YOU.

what changed for me: i went from spending an hour on scripts to 4 minutes. not exaggerating. i film more because the prep doesn't feel like a chore anymore. more videos = more sales. the math is simple.

the key: this isn't a prompt you paste in every time. it's a chat you set up ONCE with all your context — your niche, your stats, your tone, examples of your best-performing videos. then you just... talk to it. "i'm filming a video about this product, give me a hook and script." done.

2. email assistant

the problem: you have emails sitting in your inbox right now that you haven't opened. brand deals you missed. collabs you forgot to follow up on. pitches you meant to send last week.

the fix: an AI chat that sorts what matters, drafts replies, handles follow-ups and sample requests. it already knows your stats, your rates, and your tone.

but here's what makes this different from just using ChatGPT: you don't copy someone else's email templates. you BUILD your own templates WITH the assistant, using your actual numbers and your actual voice. so when a brand reaches out, your reply sounds like you wrote it at your desk — not like you found it on a blog.

what it handles:

  • cold pitches to new brands
  • warm pitches to brands you're already seeing results with
  • follow-ups when you don't hear back
  • counter offers when brands lowball you

what changed for me: i stopped losing opportunities. that's it. the emails get answered, the follow-ups happen, and i don't spend my evenings in my inbox.

3. live prep assistant

the problem: you spend an hour before every live pulling product info, thinking of talking points, trying to remember what objections people had last time. or worse — you skip the prep and wing it, and your conversion rate shows.

the fix: an AI chat that already knows your products. it pulls real reviews for objection handling, gives you talking points, engagement prompts, and saves for dead moments. you spend 2 minutes prepping instead of 60.

what changed for me: my lives got better AND i stopped dreading them. the prep was the part i hated. now it takes less time than making coffee.

the pattern

notice what all three have in common:

  • they're permanent — set up once, use forever
  • they know your context — not generic, not one-size-fits-all
  • they handle admin — the boring stuff between the creative work
  • they save time — not 5 minutes, HOURS every week
