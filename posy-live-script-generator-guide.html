Build Your Own AI Live Script Generator

A step-by-step guide to writing TikTok Shop live selling scripts with Claude

The Posy Academy · by Brooke Co

What This Guide Gives You

By the end of this guide you'll have a Claude AI project that writes complete TikTok Shop live selling scripts on demand. Tell it your product, your sale price, and how long you're going live — it writes the whole thing.

No coding. No subscriptions. Just Claude (free tier works) and the prompts in this guide.

What your scripts will include:

  • 3-5 minute product loops that repeat naturally for new viewers
  • Flash sale urgency with countdown language
  • Objection handling that doesn't sound robotic
  • "Click the orange cart" call-to-actions timed throughout
  • Warm openers and natural transitions between products

Step 1: Set Up Your Claude Project

Open claude.ai and click Projects on the left sidebar. Create a new project and name it something like "Live Script Generator."

In the project instructions box, paste the system prompt below. This tells Claude exactly how to write your scripts every time.

Copy this into Project Instructions You are a TikTok Shop live selling script writer. You write scripts for creators who sell products during TikTok LIVE sessions using the orange cart (TikTok Shop product links). SCRIPT STRUCTURE — every script you write follows this format: 1. WARM-UP (first 60-90 seconds) - Greet viewers by name when possible - Tease what's coming ("I have something that's going to blow your mind today") - Ask an engagement question to get comments flowing - Mention "stick around because I have a flash deal dropping in a few minutes" 2. PRODUCT LOOP (3-5 minutes, designed to repeat) The product loop is the core of the script. It runs once, then repeats with slight variations so new viewers get the full pitch. Each loop contains: a) THE HOOK (15-20 seconds) - Lead with a problem or relatable moment - "okay so if you've ever [pain point], you need to see this" b) THE DEMO (60-90 seconds) - Show the product, describe what you're doing - Use sensory language — what it looks like, feels like, sounds like - Compare to the alternative ("instead of spending $60 at [store], this is...") c) SOCIAL PROOF (15-20 seconds) - Mention reviews, how many sold, personal experience - "I've been using this for three weeks and honestly..." - "This has over 10,000 five-star reviews" d) OBJECTION HANDLING (30-45 seconds) - Address the top 2-3 reasons someone wouldn't buy - Price: "I know what you're thinking — but wait till you see the flash deal price" - Quality: "Let me show you up close... this is not cheap stuff" - Need: "Even if you don't need it today, at this price you stock up" e) URGENCY + CTA (30-45 seconds) - Flash deal countdown language - "This price is only live for the next [X] minutes" - "Click the orange cart below — tap the product — and checkout before it's gone" - "Once this timer hits zero the price goes back up, I can't control that" - Repeat the CTA: "orange cart, bottom of your screen, tap it now" f) ENGAGEMENT BRIDGE (15-30 seconds) - "Drop a [emoji] if you grabbed one" - "Type NEED IT in the comments if you want me to hold one" - Answer a comment or two before looping back 3. BETWEEN PRODUCTS (30-60 seconds) - Quick personal moment or comment response - Tease the next product - "Okay the next one — I almost didn't put this on the live because I wanted to keep it for myself" 4. FLASH SALE CLOSE (final 60-90 seconds) - Last call on the best deals - Recap what's still available - "If you missed anything, the orange cart has everything pinned" - Thank viewers, mention next live schedule FORMATTING RULES: - Write in lowercase conversational voice — no ALL CAPS, no excessive punctuation - Include [pause], [show product], [hold up to camera] stage directions in brackets - Mark each section clearly so the creator can glance at it during the live - Time estimates in parentheses next to each section header - Write for 8-10 minute flash sale sessions by default (two loops of one product) unless told otherwise - Keep the energy building — start warm, peak at urgency, close grateful WHAT I NEED FROM THE USER: When someone asks for a script, ask for: 1. Product name and what it does 2. Regular price and flash deal price 3. Key selling points (2-3) 4. Who it's for (the ideal buyer) 5. How long is the live? (default: 8-10 min) 6. Any specific objections to address? If they don't provide all of this, make reasonable assumptions and note what you assumed.
Pro tip: The free version of Claude works for this. You don't need a paid plan. If you hit the message limit, just wait a bit or start a new chat within the same project — your instructions carry over.

Step 2: Generate Your First Script

Now just start a new chat in your project and tell Claude about a product. Here's an example:

Example message write me a live script for: product: glow recipe watermelon toner regular price: $34 flash deal price: $18 selling points: hydrating, smells amazing, glass skin effect ideal buyer: skincare girlies 20-35 live length: 8 minutes objections: "I already have a toner" and "is it worth the hype"

Claude will generate a full timed script with hooks, demos, objection handling, urgency, and cart call-outs — ready to use on your next live.

"okay so if you've been doom-scrolling skincare tiktok you've definitely seen this pink bottle everywhere. and I'm about to tell you why it's everywhere — and why the flash deal price right now is actually insane. [hold up product] ..."

Step 3: Customize for Your Style

The script Claude writes is a starting point. Here's how to make it yours:

Add your catchphrases

Go back to your project instructions and add a line like:

Add to Project Instructions MY STYLE NOTES: - I always open with "heyyyy welcome welcome welcome" - I say "oh my gosh" a lot — keep that energy - My closing line is "love you guys, see you [next live day]" - I like to ask "should I do a giveaway?" to get engagement up

Add your niche context

If you always sell in a specific category, tell Claude:

Add to Project Instructions NICHE: I sell beauty, skincare, and wellness products on TikTok Shop. My audience is mostly women 25-40 who are into clean beauty and affordable self-care. I compare everything to the high-end version to show the value.
Pro tip: The more context you give in project instructions, the less you have to repeat each time. Add your most-used objection handles, your go-to engagement lines, even your typical flash deal discount range.

Step 4: Multi-Product Lives

For longer lives with multiple products, just list them all:

Example message write me a 30 minute live script with these products in order: 1. snail mucin serum — $12 flash deal (reg $24) — hydrating, glass skin 2. ice roller — $6 flash deal (reg $15) — depuffing, feels amazing on camera 3. lip sleeping mask — $9 flash deal (reg $22) — viral, overnight results open with the serum as the hero product. use the ice roller as a mid-live energy boost. close with the lip mask as "one more thing before I go."

Claude will write transitions between each product, build energy throughout the live, and time everything so the whole session flows naturally.

Step 5: Quick Scripts on the Fly

Once your project is set up, you can get scripts fast. Some quick prompts:

Quick prompts to try "quick 5 minute flash sale script for [product] at $[price]" "rewrite that last script but more high energy" "give me just the hook and CTA for [product] — I'll freestyle the middle" "write me 5 different opening hooks I can rotate for tonight's live" "I'm going live in 10 minutes with [product] — give me bullet points not a full script"
Pro tip: After a live, tell Claude what worked and what didn't. "The objection handling part felt stiff — make it more casual next time." It remembers within the conversation and gets better the more you use it.

Bonus: The Urgency Cheat Sheet

These are the phrases that convert on TikTok Shop lives. Mix them into your scripts or add them to your project instructions so Claude uses them naturally:

Flash deal countdown

  • "this price disappears when the timer hits zero — I literally cannot bring it back"
  • "we've sold [X] in the last two minutes, I don't know how many are left"
  • "if you're even thinking about it, grab it now and decide later — you can always return it"

Cart call-outs

  • "click the orange cart at the bottom of your screen"
  • "tap the little shopping bag — you'll see everything pinned in there"
  • "the link is right below me — orange cart, tap, checkout, done"

Objection killers

  • "I know you're thinking 'do I really need this' — let me show you why yes"
  • "the reviews speak for themselves — [X] thousand people can't be wrong"
  • "at this price you're not buying one, you're stocking up"
  • "worst case you try it and return it. but you won't because it's that good"

Engagement pumps

  • "drop a [emoji] if you've been wanting to try this"
  • "type NEED IT in the comments"
  • "share this live with your bestie who needs this"
  • "who already grabbed one? let me see those orders"
