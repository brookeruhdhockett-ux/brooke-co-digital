Our Story

More than a shop.
A community of makers.

Since 2016, The Ugly Duckling has been a home for local artisans, vintage collectors, and creative souls who believe that beautiful things deserve a second chance. We are a marketplace where sustainability meets artistry and every piece tells a story.

From repurposed furniture and antique church windows predating South Dakota statehood to hand-poured candles and custom framing, every item in our space carries the mark of someone who cares. Connected to Farmhouse Fancy, we share a passion for giving old things a fresh start.

What We Offer

Curated. Crafted. Upcycled.

Every corner of our shop holds something unexpected. Here is a taste of what you will find.

🪑

Repurposed Furniture

Dressers, tables, shelving, and statement pieces brought back to life with a fresh coat of paint, new hardware, or a creative reimagining. Each piece is one of a kind.

🏺

Vintage Finds

Antiques, curiosities, and timeless treasures sourced from estate sales, barns, and collections across the Midwest. Some pieces predate South Dakota statehood.

🖼️

Custom Framing

Preserve your memories and artwork with our custom framing services. From vintage frames to modern mats, we match the frame to the story you want to tell.

🧶

Handcrafted Goods

Locally made candles, soaps, jewelry, pottery, textiles, and more from South Dakota artisans. When you buy handcrafted, you support a neighbor.

Sustainability
